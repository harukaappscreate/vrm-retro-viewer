About

Your file never leaves your device: it is read and converted inside this page.
Drag on the screen to turn, wheel to zoom.
231 motions: Quaternius Universal Animation Library 1 & 2 and KayKit Character Animations (Kay Lousberg), all CC0, via open-vrma.
Made with Pyxel 3 by Takashi Kitao (MIT License). Runs on Pyodide (MPL-2.0), NumPy (BSD-3-Clause) and Pillow (MIT-CMU).
Tool by haruka_apps.
Sample avatar: Sendagaya Shibu, a CC0 VRoid sample model (VRM data by coati; redistribution and modification allowed). For this tool its textures were reduced and its facial expressions removed.
